Reports a call standing alone as a statement whose last result is an error that nobody looks at (errcheck, while typing): os.Open("x"), f.Close(), a method called through an interface such as io.Closer. Like errcheck by default, go and defer calls, _ = f() and the symbols of errcheck's exclude list (fmt.Print*, fmt.Fprint* to a bytes.Buffer, a strings.Builder or os.Stderr, the Write* methods of bytes.Buffer and strings.Builder, hash.Hash.Write, math/rand Read, io.Pipe*.CloseWithError) are not reported. A //nolint or //nolint:errcheck comment at the end of the line silences it, as it silences golangci-lint.

Quick fixes: Handle error (if err := f(); err != nil { return ... }, or _, err := f() with the check, returning the zero values of the enclosing function's results and the error) and Assign to blank identifier (_ = f()).
