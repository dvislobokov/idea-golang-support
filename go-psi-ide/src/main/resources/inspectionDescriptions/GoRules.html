Runs the native lint rules (ports of golangci-lint linters, staticcheck checks and revive rules) in one pass over the file. Each problem starts with the id of its rule, such as [errcheck] or [S1002]. Rules are switched on and off, and given a level and options, by the project's rule settings and its .golangci.yml.

Suppression: //nolint, //nolint:<linter> or //nolint:<rule id> at the end of a line (the whole declaration when the line starts one) or on the line above a statement or declaration; staticcheck's //lint:ignore <id> reason and //lint:file-ignore <id> reason; //noinspection GoRules for every rule. Vendored, testdata, generated and library files are not analysed.
