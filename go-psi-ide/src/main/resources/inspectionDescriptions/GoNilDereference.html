Reports a use of a variable that is nil on every path to it and panics (like vet nilness): a field selection or *x through a nil pointer, a value-receiver method called through a nil pointer, a method called on a nil interface, a write into a nil map, a call of a nil function. 
if p == nil {
    log.Print(p.Name) // p is nil here
}
A variable is known nil after x == nil held, after x = nil and after var x *T, until it is reassigned. Calls of pointer-receiver methods are legal on nil and are not reported.
