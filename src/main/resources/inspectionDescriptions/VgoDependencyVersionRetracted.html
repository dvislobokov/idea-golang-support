Reports a require of go.mod whose version its module has retracted (a retract directive in the module's latest go.mod), as go list -m -retracted module@version answers. The message quotes the rationale of the retraction.

Quick fix, when the module proxy knows a newer version: Upgrade to vX.Y.Z (go get module@version).

The check runs in the background together with the update check (Newer version of a dependency): when go.mod is opened and when its requires change, at most once an hour. It needs the network; turn this inspection off where the proxy must not be asked.
